Monster asset used on the patrol platform:
RPG Monster Duo PBR Polyart by Dungeon Mason (free)
https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/characters/creatures/rpg-monster-duo-pbr-polyart-157762
License: Standard Unity Asset Store EULA

The SlimePolyart prefab, its model, animations and texture are imported under
Assets/RPG Monster DUO PBR Polyart. The platform enemy uses that prefab as its
visible child; the PatrolStompEnemy component on the parent controls movement
and the stomp interaction. PolyartDefault.mat was adapted to URP for this game.
